The Candidate

From Middletown
to the White House

JD Vance grew up in the working-class hills of Appalachian Ohio. He watched his community hollowed out by deindustrialisation and opioid addiction — and he never forgot where he came from.

He served as a US Marine, earned a Yale law degree, wrote the bestselling memoir Hillbilly Elegy, won the Ohio Senate seat in 2022, and served as Donald Trump's 2024 running mate. Now he's running to Make America Greater.

The Platform

Six Pillars of Make America Greater

A bold, clear agenda for working families, national security, and American sovereignty.

🏭
01

Rebuild American Manufacturing

Bring jobs home. Tariffs on China. Tax incentives for domestic production. Revive the Rust Belt and every community that globalism abandoned.

🛡️
02

Secure the Border — For Good

Finish the wall. End catch-and-release. Deport criminal aliens. Zero amnesty. America's border is a national security matter — full stop.

💰
03

Economic Freedom

Cut taxes on working families. Slash regulatory red tape. Stop shipping American wealth overseas. Put Main Street ahead of Wall Street.

🎓
04

Education for Families

School choice. Vocational training. End left-wing indoctrination. Return curriculum control to parents and local communities — not federal bureaucrats.

⚖️
05

Law & Order

Back the blue. Fund police. Mandatory sentencing for violent crime. Shut down the radical DA experiment destroying American cities.

🌍
06

America First Foreign Policy

No more endless wars. No more nation-building. Strong military. American interests first in every negotiation — not globalist institutions.
